Frequently asked questions

Are you NICEIC approved?
Yes — Voltline is an NICEIC approved contractor, so every job meets the latest UK safety standards and comes with proper certification.

We do — for both homeowners and landlords. We carry out the inspection, provide the certificate, and can fix any issues found.
Can you install an EV charger?
Absolutely. We supply and install approved home and workplace EV charge points and handle all the registration for you.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes — every quote is free and fixed, so you’ll know the exact price before any work begins.
What areas do you cover?
Richmond, Twickenham, Kew, Isleworth, Brentford and the surrounding West London areas. Just ask if you’re nearby.
