What we build

Services for care providers.

Websites, business apps, AI assistants and automation — four straightforward things, chosen to suit your service.

Care websites

A clear site for families, commissioners and prospective staff — About, Services, Referrals, Careers and Safeguarding, mobile-first and Google-ready. From £995.

Business apps

Simplicity for policies and procedures, Safer Recruitment for hiring, Restraint Log for physical interventions — practical systems, not more paperwork.

AI assistants

An AI receptionist that answers common questions, takes messages and routes anything important to a person — never for safeguarding or emergency advice.

Automation

Automatic reminders for policy acknowledgements, DBS renewals, right-to-work checks and training due dates — so nothing slips.

Examples

What we could build for you.

Pick the parts that fit — most projects are a website plus one or two of the below.

  • A website that shows families what your service does, with Referrals and Careers pages ready for Google.
  • Safer Recruitment: candidates, references, DBS and right-to-work in one managed workflow.
  • Simplicity: policies with acknowledgements, version history and staff reminders built in.
  • Restraint Log that replaces paper books, with clear sign-offs and a review workflow.
  • An AI receptionist that handles after-hours calls without stepping anywhere near safeguarding.
