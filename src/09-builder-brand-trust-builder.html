01

About us

A warm, confident introduction to who you are.

Marshall & Sons is a family-run building company serving West London and Surrey for over 30 years. From extensions and loft conversions to full renovations and new builds, we take on every project with the same care we’d give our own homes.

We’ve grown almost entirely on word of mouth — because we turn up when we say we will, keep our sites clean, and finish what we start. No drama, no disappearing acts. Just honest, quality building, done properly.

02

Founder / business story

The human story behind the company builds instant connection and trust.

It started in 1992 with Dave Marshall, a van, and a simple belief: do good work, treat people right, and the rest takes care of itself. Three decades on, Dave still answers the phone — now alongside his two sons, who learned the trade on his sites.

We’re still that same family firm at heart. We’ve just got a bigger team, a longer list of happy customers, and a reputation across the area we’re very proud to protect.

Guarantee wording

Clear guarantee copy that removes the risk for the customer.

Every project we complete comes with a written workmanship guarantee. If anything isn’t right within the guarantee period, we’ll come back and put it right — no quibbles, no extra cost. That’s our name on it, and we don’t take that lightly.

Safety & compliance wording

Reassures customers that everything is done by the book.

All our work is carried out to current Building Regulations and health & safety standards. We’re fully insured with public liability cover, our team holds the relevant trade qualifications, and we handle the necessary certifications and sign-offs so you don’t have to worry about a thing.

Customer reassurance wording

Directly addresses the fears that stop people booking a builder.

Worried about cowboy builders? So are we. That’s why everything is fixed-price, in writing, and guaranteed.

Worried about mess and disruption? We protect your home, tidy up daily, and keep you updated every step of the way.

Worried we’ll vanish halfway through? We’ve been here 30 years and we’ll be here long after your project’s done.

10

Case study structure

A reusable framework that turns finished jobs into powerful proof. Fill it in for each project.

The brief
What the client wanted.e.g. “A double-storey side extension to create a kitchen-diner and extra bedroom.”
The challenge
Any tricky bit you solved.e.g. “Working around a tight access lane and keeping the family living there throughout.”
What we did
The work, in plain English.e.g. “Full build, electrics, plastering and finishing over 10 weeks, on schedule.”
The result
The outcome and value.e.g. “A bright, open family space — and an estimated 15% added to the home’s value.”
Client quote
One line in their words.e.g. “They made a stressful project genuinely easy.”
