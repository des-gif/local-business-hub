04 · PRODUCTION OPTIONS

AI presenter & caption styling

AI presenter / avatar option

  • No footage? The same script can be delivered by a friendly AI presenter / avatar.
  • Suggested style: warm, approachable, smart-casual — speaking directly to camera.
  • Pair the avatar with simple b-roll or bold text scenes for energy.
  • Ideal for businesses that want a polished video fast, without a film crew.

Caption & text-overlay guidance

  • Bold, condensed, UPPERCASE font (like this one) for punch.
  • Keep overlays to 3–5 words, lower-third or centred.
  • High-contrast lime-on-dark so it’s readable on mobile.
  • Animate text in quickly on each scene change — assume sound is off.
