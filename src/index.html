What we build

Four straightforward things,
built around your business.

Websites

A website that brings customers to your door.

Your website is often the first thing a customer sees, usually on their phone, often late in the evening. In a few seconds, it needs to show what you do, prove you're good at it, and make it easy to get in touch.

We build fast, mobile-first websites with clear pages for each service, real photos of your work, reviews, the areas you cover, and an enquiry or quote form that asks the right questions. An electrician's website could let customers request a quote and upload photos of the job, so you know what you're dealing with before you call back.

We also set it up so local customers find you on Google. You get a website that works for you around the clock, even when you're busy.

For an electrician: quote requests with job photos.

Business apps

Apps that keep your work organised.

Most small businesses run on a mix of notebooks, spreadsheets, emails and memory. It works, until something gets missed. A business app puts everything in one place and shows you what needs attention.

A builder could see every enquiry, turn it into a quote, then a job, then an invoice, and know at a glance what's been paid. A care provider could keep recruitment documents, DBS checks, training records and policy sign-offs in one place, ready for inspection.

We build simple apps around the way you already work, so your team actually uses them. You can start small, with one app, and add more as you grow.

For a care provider: recruitment and training records.

AI assistants

AI assistants that help with everyday enquiries.

An AI assistant is like a friendly receptionist who never goes home. It answers common questions on your website or phone using only information you've approved: your services, prices, opening hours, areas you cover. It collects the customer's details and passes the enquiry to you, neatly summarised.

A salon assistant could explain treatments and prices, then send customers straight to the booking page. You stay in control of what it says, and anything sensitive, urgent or complicated always goes to a real person.

Try ours: it's the same kind of assistant we can build for your business.

For a salon: service information and booking links.

Automation

Automation that takes care of the chasing.

So much time goes on reminders and follow-ups. Automation does it for you, quietly in the background. Appointment reminders that cut no-shows. Polite follow-ups on quotes that haven't been answered. Requests for missing documents. Payment reminders, so you're paid on time. Review requests, so happy customers help you win new ones. Renewal reminders for services, checks and certificates.

You set the rules once, and the system gets on with it. Your team spends less time repeating the same tasks and more time helping customers.

For a builder: automatic quote follow-ups.
