AI Customer Response System

Never lose a customer to a slow reply

Prepared for: RoadReady Mobile Mechanics · covering West London
🔧 Mobile mechanic / garage 💬 SMS · WhatsApp · email ⚡ Copy, tweak, send
01

New enquiry replies

Fast, friendly first responses that capture the details you need.

First replyText

Hi [Name], thanks for getting in touch with RoadReady! 🔧 Happy to help — pop me your car’s make, model and reg, plus what’s happening with it, and I’ll come back with a price and the soonest I can get to you.

Out-of-hours auto-replyText

Thanks for your message! I’m out on jobs right now but I’ll get back to you first thing. If it’s an urgent breakdown, give me a call on [number].

02

Missed call messages

Turn a missed call into a booking before they ring the next garage.

Missed call follow-upText

Hi [Name], sorry I missed your call — I was under a bonnet! 🔧 Ring me back on [number] or text me what you need and I’ll get you sorted.

After-hours missed callText

Hi, sorry I couldn’t grab your call just now. I’ll be free to talk after [time] — or reply here with what’s up and I’ll help straight away.

03

Quote follow-ups

A gentle nudge keeps quotes from going cold — most jobs are won on the follow-up.

First follow-upText

Hi [Name], just checking you got the quote for your [car]? Happy to answer any questions or get you booked in — just say the word. 👍

Second follow-upText

Hi [Name], still happy to help with your [car] whenever suits you. I’ve a couple of slots free this week if you’d like one before they go?

04

Booking confirmations

Clear confirmations that cut no-shows and build trust.

ConfirmationText

You’re booked in, [Name]! ✅ [Service] on [date] at [time], at [address]. I’ll text when I’m on my way. If anything changes, just let me know.

Day-before reminderText

Hi [Name], quick reminder I’m booked to see your [car] tomorrow at [time]. Please make sure I can access it. See you then! 🔧

05

Review requests

Ask while they’re happy — right after a job well done.

Review requestText

Hi [Name], hope the [car]’s running sweet! 🚗 If you’ve a sec, a quick Google review really helps a small local business like mine: [link]. Cheers!

Review request — emailEmail

Hi [Name], thanks again for trusting RoadReady with your [car]. Reviews make a huge difference to a one-man operation — if you were happy, I’d be really grateful for a quick review here: [link]. Thanks! — [Your name]

06

Payment reminders

Get paid on time without the awkwardness — a friendly nudge first, a firmer one if needed.

Gentle reminderText

Hi [Name], hope all’s well and the [car]’s running great! Just a friendly nudge that the invoice for your recent work ([amount]) is due. You can pay by [methods]. Thanks!

Firmer reminderEmail

Hi [Name], just following up on invoice [#] for [amount], now [x] days overdue. Could you settle it by [date]? If there’s any problem, let me know and we’ll sort something out. Thanks, [Your name].

07

Customer reactivation

Win back past customers and remind them when they’re due — easy repeat business.

Service-due reminderText

Hi [Name], it’s been about a year since I last looked at your [car] — it may be due a service! Want me to pop you in the diary? Returning customers get [offer]. 🔧

We miss youText

Hi [Name], just checking in — how’s the [car] doing? If it needs anything, you know where I am. Always happy to help my regulars first. 👍

08

Complaint responses

Handle problems calmly and quickly — it’s how good reputations are protected.

First responseText

Hi [Name], I’m really sorry to hear that — that’s not the service I want to give. I’d like to put it right; can you give me a call on [number] so I can understand what’s happened and sort it for you?

Resolution follow-upEmail

Hi [Name], thanks for talking it through with me. As promised, [what you’ll do]. I really appreciate your patience and the chance to make it right — your trust means a lot.

How to use these well

  • Reply fast — even a holding text beats silence.
  • Always use their first name and their car.
  • Keep it warm and human; light emojis are fine.
  • Save them as phone shortcuts to send in seconds.
